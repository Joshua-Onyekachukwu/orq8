# ORQ8 — The AI Organization Operating System

**One-liner:** ORQ8 lets a human CEO hire an AI workforce, organize it into departments and teams, govern it with a constitution and approval system, and receive executive reporting — while every action is budgeted, audited, and explainable.

**Tagline:** *Tell ORQ8 what you want. It hires the team, does the work, and reports back.*

---

## Problem

Agentic AI is the fastest-growing software category — but **Gartner predicts over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027** due to *escalating costs, unclear business value, and inadequate risk controls* — management problems, not model problems. Meanwhile 41% of enterprises rolled back a production agent last year over reliability. Everyone is building agents; **nobody is building the operating system that makes agents safe, governed, and accountable** — and solo operators drowning in ops have no one to delegate to at all.

## Solution

ORQ8 is a programmable organization, not a chatbot. The CEO speaks vaguely ("I think there's a business here — investigate it"); the Executive Agent forms a council, researches, debates, and returns a recommendation with evidence and required approval — then executes through departments of hired AI employees, each with explicit authority, budget, and model policy. **Golden Workflow:** paste an idea → council-reviewed recommendation → executed validation plan run by a temporary AI team → audited, reported, learned.

- **Governance in code, not prompts** — constitution, approval tiers, financial controls, tamper-evident audit trail
- **Company memory & decision precedent** — institutional continuity that survives agent replacement
- **CEO attention protection** — a Decision Center, not an agent zoo
- **FOSS-first & model-agnostic** — runs on free local models today (Ollama), BYOK for frontier models, no lock-in

## Business model

Price the platform; **pass through model costs (BYOK)** — users pay for their own tokens. Free (1 org, 3 agents) · **Pro $49/mo** (10 agents, BYOK, governance) · **Business $199/mo** (50 agents, audit, integrations) · Enterprise custom (private deployment, SLA).

## Competitive moat

Vertical agents (Sierra, 11x) do one job with no org-wide governance; agent builders (Lindy, Relevance) give users *more agents and more noise*; big platforms (OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft, Salesforce) are platform-locked. **No one owns governance + memory + attention protection as a neutral, model-agnostic organization OS.** Memory and audit create switching costs; the wedge market is uncontested.
