# ADR-013 — KPIs as a First-Class Table

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08 · **Scope:** Strategy/domain

## Context
The domain model (05) lists KPI as an entity, but an early schema draft embedded KPIs as `objectives.kpis jsonb` — duplicated modeling.

## Decision
KPIs live in a **`kpis` table** (objective_id, name, metric_key, target, current_value, unit, source, status). `objectives.kpis jsonb` is dropped.

## Consequences
- KPIs are queryable and feed metric_snapshots, goal health, and reports (16, 40).
- Cleaner joins from tasks/projects to their KPI contribution.
