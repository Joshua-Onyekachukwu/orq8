# ADR-021 — No AI-Agent Marketplace; Platform-Led Revenue

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-08 · **Scope:** Business model / product positioning

## Context
The master brief floated an "AI team marketplace" and premium agent templates as potential additional revenue (§78). The product decision overrides that suggestion: ORQ8 must not become an agent broker, and must never take commissions on agents.

## Decision
1. **ORQ8 does not operate an AI-agent marketplace.** Agents are hired *within an organization* when a business need exists, and are assigned to departments, teams, projects, and responsibilities.
2. **Hiring initiation:** the User (CEO) or an authorized executive/department agent may initiate hiring, in line with organizational authority and budget policies. Agents propose hires via business cases; they never self-hire and hiring approval follows the authority model (18) and constitution defaults (17b).
3. **Revenue model:** ORQ8 earns from the organization operating system itself — platform tiers, organizational capacity, enterprise capabilities, and optional AI/voice/infrastructure usage — **not from agent sales, agent commissions, or marketplace cuts**.

## Consequences
- Docs 01 (§1.8), 55 (§55.5), 00 (§6), and the investor one-pager are updated to reflect this.
- The "internal marketplace" feature (27.4) remains a **discovery surface** for the organization's own tools and capabilities ("do we already have something that extracts PDF data?") — not a third-party agent store.
- Integration extensions and internal-tool sharing stay platform features; no revenue line is tied to selling agents.
