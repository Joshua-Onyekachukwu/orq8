- ✱ **Propose-first** — drafts options, acts only within explicit authority
- **Ask-first** — never acts without you except continuing approved tasks
- **Act-first within caps** — proceeds and reports if cost/authority fits

**A2. Initiative loop depth (uninterrupted steps before a digest)?**
- ✱ 5 steps · 3 steps · 8 steps · founder-set per goal

**A3. EA initiative budget — unprompted small actions per day?**
- ✱ **0 Cr (disabled at launch)** · 100 Cr/day · 250 Cr/day · founder-set
- When exhausted: ✱ Atlas proposes instead of acting.

**A4. May Atlas run initiative loops overnight / while you're away?**
- ✱ Yes, within caps, digest ready in the morning · No, quiet hours 22:00–07:00
