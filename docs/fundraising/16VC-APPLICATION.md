## Company

- **Startup name:** ORQ8
- **Website / landing page:** `[FILL IN — https://orq8.com when live, else https://orq8.vercel.app]`
- **What are you building?**
  ORQ8 is an AI-native organizational operating system: instead of hiring a
  chatbot, a founder hires a structured AI workforce — departments, teams, and
  AI employees organized like a real company — and delegates objectives to an
  Executive Agent that plans, delegates, executes, and coordinates across the
  whole organization, with the founder approving consequential decisions.
- **The problem & solution:**
  AI tools today are copilots for individuals: they answer, they don't
  *operate*. A solo founder still juggles a dozen SaaS tools and does all the
  coordination themselves. ORQ8 replaces the org chart-in-a-box problem: the
  company's structure — Executive Office, Engineering, Marketing, Sales,
  Finance and 18 more catalog departments — lives in the product, each with
  recommended teams and AI employees hired in one click from a stage-appropriate
  catalog. Work flows top-down (objectives → plans → tasks → execution), and
  intelligence flows bottom-up (outcomes → evaluations → performance signals →
  better future decisions).
- **Why now?**
  Frontier-model APIs made specialized reasoning affordable per-task in the
  last 18 months; agentic tool-use became reliable enough to act, not just
  advise; and structured multi-agent orchestration — not more model cleverness
  — is now the bottleneck. The missing layer is organizational: who decides,
  who executes, who approves, who learns. That layer didn't exist two years ago
  because the model quality didn't justify organizing it.

- **The "secret":**
  An AI company needs the same thing a human company needs — an organizational
  structure that turns individual intelligence into institutional capability.
  Everyone else is bolting agents onto chat windows; ORQ8 starts from the
  org chart. The catalog of 23 department templates with stage-appropriate
  staffing is the wedge: a founder describes their company, ORQ8 recommends the
  lean set of functions they need *now* (and defers the rest with reasons), and
  they can hire the workforce in one click. That structure is what makes
  delegation, deliberation, approval, and learning coherent — it's also a real
  moat, because the organizational memory and performance history accumulate
  inside the structure.
- **What do you understand that others don't? (≤150 words)**
  The unit of AI progress that matters for founders isn't a smarter model — it's
  a *structured* one. A single model asked to "run my company" fails not because
  it can't reason but because it has no role separation, no accountability, no
  memory of who predicted what. Humans solved this with organizations: separate
  roles, deliberation, approval gates, post-mortems. ORQ8 ports that
  institutional machinery to AI labor — and it measurably works: decisions get
  expected outcomes, actual outcomes are compared against them, and per-agent
  and per-model performance signals update from measured history, so the
  organization's future recommendations are informed by what actually happened.
  Structure is the product.
