### The $500K SAFE Trap

| If You Raise | If You Bootstrap |
|-------------|-----------------|

| 5-7.5% dilution | 0% dilution |

| Investor expectations | Your own timeline |
| Board meetings | Build, ship, repeat |
| Monthly updates to investors | Ship features, not slides |
| Pressure to grow at ALL costs | Grow at YOUR pace |

### The Real Cost of $500K

If you raise $500K and give away 7.5%:
- At $10M exit: you just lost $750K
- At $100M exit: you just lost $7.5M
- At $1B exit: you just lost $75M

**The best fundraising decision is to not need one.**

### When to Raise (If Ever)

Raise ONLY when:
1. You have proven product-market fit ($50K+ MRR)
2. You have a clear path to $10M ARR
3. You need capital to capture a time-sensitive market
4. The capital will accelerate growth by 3-5x (not 1.5x)
5. You're turning away customers because of capacity

**Right now: none of these apply. Build the engine first.**

When you raise $500K:
1. You spend 2-3 months fundraising (not building)

6. You run out of runway before product-market fit
7. You do a "bridge round" at bad terms

**The best way to avoid fundraising is to not need it.**
