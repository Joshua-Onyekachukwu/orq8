# ORQ8 — Design Partner Outreach Kit

**Status:** Draft for review · **Sources:** docs/00 §5 (wedge), §5.6 (monetization Stage 1), §7 (GTM) · 04_GOLDEN_WORKFLOW (hero offer) · ADR-021 (no marketplace)
**Goal:** Recruit the first **3–5 solo-founder design partners** for free, in exchange for validation, feedback, and a testimonial.
**Application form:** marketing/design_partner_application.md — point candidates here ([orq8.ai/design-partners]) instead of scheduling cold.
**2-week execution plan:** marketing/design_partner_plan.md — which communities to hit first, posting + DM cadence, funnel targets, stop rules.
**Placeholders** marked [bracketed] — personalize before sending. Never send unpersonalized.

---

## 0. Who to contact (targeting)

**The perfect design partner is a solo founder who:**
- Runs a real business or real product solo (not an idea-stage lurker) — they have *actual decisions* to feed ORQ8
- Feels the ops load personally ("too much to do, no one to delegate to")
- Is AI-curious but wary — wants *control* and *accountability*, not another chatbot
- Has ~30 minutes/week for a feedback call and will honestly tell you what's broken

**Where to find them (best first):**
1. **Indie Hackers** — solo founders are the entire audience; search "solo founder" posts, comment genuinely first, then DM
2. **X/Twitter** — people posting about building alone, AI agents, or "I'm a one-person company"; engage publicly before DMing
3. **LinkedIn** — founders of 1–3 person companies; the "zero-employee company" discourse is hot there
4. **Discord/Slack communities** — AI builder servers, indie SaaS communities
5. **Your own network** — friends of friends who started something alone (warm intros convert 10× better)

**Anti-targets (skip):** people who "just want to try AI tools," agency owners (Phase 2+ market — don't spend design-partner slots on them), anyone who won't commit to feedback.

---

## 1. The offer (what you're giving and asking)

**Give (free, no card):**
- Full Pro-equivalent access for the design-partner period (~6 months, through beta)
- A direct line to the founder — your roadmap is their roadmap; their feedback actually ships
- Early access before anyone else; naming rights in the changelog if they want

**Ask (what makes it a *design partnership*, not a free trial):**
- Use ORQ8 for one real business decision in the first two weeks (not a demo — a real one)
- One 30-minute onboarding call + one feedback call per month
- Honest criticism: what's broken, confusing, or missing
- Permission to use their story as a case study / testimonial (anonymized if preferred)

**Slots:** we're keeping this to 3–5 people on purpose — it's a partnership, not a waitlist.

## 2. Cold DM (X/Twitter or LinkedIn) — primary

> **Subject/nothing — DMs don't need one. First line is everything.**

Hi [FirstName] — saw your post about [specific thing: building [product] solo / your one-person company note]. Genuinely curious about [one specific detail you noticed].

I'm building ORQ8 — an operating system for one-person companies. You tell it what you want ("I think there's a business here — investigate it"), and it hires a temporary AI team: a council researches and debates, gives you a recommendation with evidence, you approve, and it executes and reports back. Governance, budgets, and an audit trail are built in — you stay the CEO, the system runs the org.

I'm looking for **3–5 solo founders to be design partners** — free access for ~6 months in exchange for using it on one real decision and telling me what breaks. Not a sales pitch; I genuinely want to build this *with* people living the problem.

Worth a 20-minute call this week?

## 4. Cold email — full template

**Subject lines (pick one):**
- `An operating system for your one-person company`
- `You build alone — I built you a team`
- `3–5 design partners wanted (solo founders)`
- `Your company, staffed by AI — you stay CEO`

**Body:**

Hi [FirstName],

You build [product] alone — I know what that means: ideas, customers, and a to-do list that never ends, with no one to delegate to.

I'm building ORQ8 to fix exactly that. It's not another AI chatbot or agent dashboard. It's an **operating system for a company staffed by AI employees**:

- You give it a vague instruction — *"I think there's a business here, investigate it"* — and it figures out intent
- A **council of AI employees** researches independently, debates, and returns a recommendation with evidence, alternatives, and confidence
- You approve in a **Decision Center** (or reject, or modify) — nothing consequential happens without you
- It **hires temporary AI employees**, executes the plan, tracks every dollar, and reports back weekly
- A **Company Constitution** enforces what the org will never do — approval tiers and spending limits are enforced in code, not by prompt

I'm keeping the first cohort small on purpose: **3–5 solo founders as design partners.** In exchange for free access through beta (~6 months), I ask that you use it on one real business decision, and tell me honestly what's broken — your feedback ships, not just gets collected.

If you're open to a 20-minute call this week, reply and I'll send a calendar link. Either way, [one-line genuine sign-off tied to their work].

Best,
[Your name]
[Founder, ORQ8 · orq8.ai]

> **Send as:** plain text with `ORQ8_DESIGN_PARTNER.pdf` attached (or a link to it) — the one-pager does the heavy lifting, so the email stays short.

## 6. The 20-minute call (what to actually do)

1. **Listen first (10 min):** ask what they'd delegate first if they had a team, what they're spending time on weekly, what they've tried with AI and why it failed
2. **Show the Golden Workflow (5 min):** take *their* actual idea/problem and walk the path — idea → council → recommendation → approval → execution → report. Don't demo a canned example
3. **Set the agreement (5 min):** one real decision in the first two weeks, one feedback call a month, testimonial permission at the end. Confirm the free period and what happens after
4. **Send a same-day recap email** with what you agreed — it's the first proof that ORQ8's founder runs things like a system

---

## 7. Qualify on the call (do NOT skip)

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Has a real business/product today? | Yes — not idea-stage |
| Has a real decision to feed ORQ8 in 2 weeks? | Names one concrete decision |
| Understands it's early & will tolerate rough edges? | Says yes out loud |
| Will commit to feedback calls? | Agrees to a schedule |
| OK being a public reference (or anonymous)? | Either is fine, must be explicit |

**3–5 passes = cohort full. Don't fudge it** — a design partner who doesn't use the product is worse than no partner (they dilute your signal and your story).
