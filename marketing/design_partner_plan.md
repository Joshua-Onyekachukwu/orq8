# ORQ8 — Design Partner Outreach Plan (2 Weeks)

**Status:** Draft for review · **Sources:** docs/00 §5 (wedge), §7.1 (Stage 0: 3–5 design partners, free) · marketing/design_partner_outreach.md (§0 channels, §2–§5 message templates, §7 gates) · design_partner_application.md (form)
**Goal:** fill the **3–5 solo-founder cohort** in 14 days — qualified, not just interested.
**Time budget:** ~30 min/day (≈7 hours total). This is a numbers game with a personal touch; the plan front-loads the channels where solo founders actually are.
**Placeholders** marked [bracketed] — swap in real names/links before sending.

## 1. The funnel (what "working" looks like)

| Stage | Target volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Reach (posts + DMs) | 50–70 DMs + 4–6 posts | Posts reach hundreds; DMs are the conversion engine |
| Replies | 10–15 | Cold DM reply rate ~8–15% on a genuinely personalized line |
| Calls booked | 5–7 | 30–40% of repliers agree to the 20-minute call |
| **Cohort (accepted)** | **3–5** | ~50% of calls pass the §7 gates; stop at 5 |

**Daily rhythm:** 15 min posts/engagement (morning) + 15 min DMs (midday, their timezone). Do not skip the engagement step — a DM after a genuine public interaction converts ~3× better.

## 4. Rules that keep it honest

1. **Personalize the first line or don't send it.** One specific observation about *them* (their post, product, or problem) — the rest of the DM can be the template.
2. **One follow-up, day 3–4, then stop** (outreach §5). A no-reply is a no.
3. **Qualify on the call, not the DM** — run the §7 checklist; 5/5 passes = in. No fudging.
4. **Never send unpersonalized DMs in batches > 5.** Above that, it reads as spam and burns the account.
5. **Everyone who applies gets a reply** — yes, no, or "not yet" (keep the warm ones for the beta waitlist).

## 5. Success at day 14

Per docs/00 §7.1: **3–5 active design partners** · ≥1 real Golden Workflow decision started per partner · ≥1 public testimonial permission · 10+ pieces of actionable feedback logged · zero signups who ghost. If you're at 2 partners but they're *great*, that beats 5 who never log in — quality gates first, volume second.
