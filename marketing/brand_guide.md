## 1. The brand platform

| Element | Canonical form |
|---|---|
| **Name** | ORQ8 — always uppercase, pronounced **"or-kate"** (orchestrate + organization). Never "ORQ eight." |
| **Positioning statement** | *The AI Organization Operating System — not another agent dashboard. An operating system.* |
| **Tagline** | *"Tell ORQ8 what you want. It hires the team, does the work, and reports back."* |
| **Hero message** | *"Paste an idea → researched, council-reviewed recommendation → an executed validation plan run by a temporary AI team — every dollar tracked, every decision explainable."* |
| **Anti-message** | *"Not a chatbot. Not a task manager. Not an agent zoo. An operating system for a company staffed by AI."* |

## 2. Positioning — how we win

- **Category:** the operating system for an organization staffed by AI employees — the governance layer everyone else is missing (Gartner: 40%+ of agentic AI projects canceled by 2027 on cost, unclear value, missing risk controls).
- **Audience:** the solo founder / indie operator / one-person company (wedge). Agencies and SMBs come later — same platform, no rebuild.
- **Differentiation:** human sovereignty (CEO is the final authority) · governance in code, not prompts · company memory & decision precedent · money & budget control · tamper-evident audit trail · model-agnostic (free local models or BYOK, no markup, no lock-in) · no commissions, no marketplace.
- **We are not:** an agent builder, a chatbot, a task manager, an agent dashboard, an AI-agent marketplace (ADR-021).

## 3. Voice & tone — calm, executive, plain

Like a competent COO writing to the CEO — confident, understated, concrete. Outcomes over "AI activity."

- Speak to the CEO as the authority; never imply ORQ8 replaces them.
- Concrete over abstract: *"a temporary strategy council researches and debates"* — not "multi-agent reasoning."
- No hype, no fear-mongering, no overpromising. Numbers when you claim them.
- Agents are *employees of your organization* — hire, onboard, review, replace. Never "bots running loose."

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| an operating system for your company | an AI platform / an agent platform |
| AI employees / your AI team | bots, agent zoo, swarms |
| governance in code | "safe prompts" / guardrails by prompt |
| you decide, the system executes | autonomous AI running your business |
| every dollar tracked | vague "savings" claims |
| a temporary AI team for this project | mercenary / gig agents |
| your keys, no markup, no commissions | free AI, unlimited AI |

## 4. Message hierarchy

1. **Sovereignty:** You stay the CEO. ORQ8 runs the organization.
2. **Promise:** Tell ORQ8 what you want — it hires the team, does the work, reports back.
3. **Proof:** Governance, budgets, memory, and a full audit trail, enforced in code.
4. **Risk removal:** Free to start. Free/local models. Your keys, no markup, no commissions.

## 5. Brand guardrails

- **Calm, executive design** (33) — no card walls, no confetti, no "look what AI did" energy.
- **Human sovereignty** — the CEO is the final authority, always.
- **No marketplace, no commissions** (ADR-021) — sell the operating system, never agents.
- **FOSS-first & model-agnostic** — no lock-in, no vendor shilling.
- **Outcomes over activity** — judge by results and dollars, not tokens or agent counts.
- **Identity:** register `orq8.ai` (or `.app`/`.dev`), claim `@orq8` early; brand is uncontested.
