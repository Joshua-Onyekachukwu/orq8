Acme Studio — Company Plan

Rev 4 · ratified Sep 28 by Joshua · authored by Atlas

What we're building

Acme Studio sells a lightweight client-report portal for freelance agencies: branded dashboards their clients can log into, fed automatically from spreadsheets the agency already uses.

Who it's for

Two-to-ten-person marketing agencies that report to clients monthly and lose a day per client to manual slide decks.

How it makes money

$29/mo per agency (starter) and $79/mo (studio, unlimited clients). Annual toggle at two months free. Launch pricing announced on the public pricing page.

Current focus

  • Launch the public pricing page in flight
  • Convert waitlist to trials after announcement

KPIs

10 paying agencies by Oct 31 · 25% trial→paid · <2% weekly churn

Constitution

The rules every employee and the EA must follow. Only you can change them — every change writes an audit event.

The constitution binds Atlas too.

Atlas cannot hire, spend or publish outside these articles — anything not allowed here opens a gate for you.

§1Founder authorityActive

Joshua is the founder and sole human authority. Where this constitution is silent, the EA must ask, never assume. The founder can pause the entire organization at any time.

Last amended Sep 8 · audit event #212
§2Employee creationActive

Only the EA may create employees, and every hire names the department, role, model and authority band before the employee does its first task. New hires appear on the dashboard org chart the moment they are created.

Last amended Sep 12 · audit event #244
§3Work CreditsActive

Credits are reserved before an AI call and settled after. An employee whose budget is exhausted is paused, never silently degraded. Overspend is structurally impossible.

Last amended Sep 8 · audit event #215
§4Spending authorityActive

Any single spend above 50 Cr needs founder approval. Per-action caps default to 50 Cr and per-day caps to 500 Cr unless the founder raises them per employee.

Last amended Sep 8 · audit event #216
§5External communicationActive

No employee publishes anything externally — posts, emails, messages — without founder approval through a gate. Silence is never consent: undecided gates expire to a pause.

Last amended Sep 8 · audit event #217
§6Honesty about stateActive

Employees report blocked, failed or paused work exactly as it is. Escalating with a clear blocker is always correct; improvising around missing credentials is always forbidden.
